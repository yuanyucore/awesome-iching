# Awesome I Ching (Book of Changes) [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of awesome I Ching (易經) resources, digital tools, software libraries, translations, and philosophical guides.

The I Ching, or **Book of Changes**, is an ancient Chinese divination text and philosophical system. It is structured around 64 hexagrams—combinations of six broken (Yin) and unbroken (Yang) lines—which represent the dynamic states of change in the universe.

*For an interactive, digital I Ching consultation tool and detailed line interpretations, visit [Yuanyu](https://www.yuanyucore.com).*

## Digital Divination Tools

*Websites and applications for casting hexagrams and reading interpretations.*

*   [Yuanyu I Ching Oracle](https://www.yuanyucore.com) - A modern, interactive I Ching tool that guides you through the coin-toss casting method, generates changing lines, and provides clear, practical interpretations of the resulting hexagrams.
*   [I Ching Online](https://www.ichingonline.net/) - A long-standing, simple virtual coin-shaking tool for quick readings.
*   [iYarrow](https://iyarrow.xyz) - A free web app pairing I Ching hexagram casting with Zi Wei Dou Shu (紫微斗數) natal charts. Casting uses a visible-entropy interaction (fill a star field, touch three times) fed into an open-source 梅花易數 (Plum Blossom) casting engine, in 6 locales.

## Developer Tools & Libraries

*Open-source code, APIs, and data files representing the 64 hexagrams and trigrams.*

*   [iching (Python Package)](https://pypi.org/project/iching/) - A Python library designed to simulate I Ching divination, generate hexagrams, and fetch associated texts.
*   [i-ching (npm Package)](https://www.npmjs.com/package/i-ching) - A Node.js module to programmatically roll the I Ching using either the yarrow stalk or three-coin method.
*   [Unicode I Ching Hexagrams Symbols](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Ching_hexagram_symbols) - Information on the dedicated Unicode block (U+4DC0 to U+4DFF) containing all 64 hexagram characters for developers.
*   [meihua-yishu](https://github.com/muyen/meihua-yishu) - An open-source implementation of the 梅花易數 (Plum Blossom) casting method, packaged as a divination skill for Claude/ChatGPT/Gemini/DeepSeek-style LLM agents.

## Educational Resources

*Articles, guides, and diagrams explaining the core concepts of the Book of Changes.*

*   [The 8 Trigrams (Bagua) Explained](https://www.yuanyucore.com/learn-trigrams) - A foundational guide to the eight trigrams (Heaven, Earth, Water, Fire, Wind, Thunder, Lake, Mountain) and their elemental associations.
*   [How to Cast the I Ching](https://www.yuanyucore.com/how-to-cast) - A step-by-step tutorial comparing the traditional Yarrow Stalk method and the 3-Coin method.
*   [The Structure of a Hexagram](https://www.yuanyucore.com/hexagram-structure) - An article explaining how to read hexagrams from the bottom line (first line) to the top line (sixth line).

## Translations & Classical Commentaries

*Highly regarded translations and traditional commentaries on the text.*

*   *The I Ching, or Book of Changes* (Wilhelm-Baynes Translation) - One of the most famous and widely read English translations, featuring a foreword by Carl Jung.
*   *The Tao of I Ching* by Tsung Hwa Jou - A comprehensive look at the history, math, and philosophy behind the system.
*   *The Ten Wings (十翼)* - The traditional collection of commentaries on the I Ching, historically attributed to Confucius and his disciples, which explains the philosophical depths of the hexagrams.
